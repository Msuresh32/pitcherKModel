Research protocol

What the numbers do—and do not—mean

Architecture
Ensemble of Poisson GLM, XGBoost Poisson, LightGBM Poisson, histogram gradient-boosted Poisson, and CatBoost Poisson. Count means are converted to prop probabilities with a Negative-Binomial tail.
H0 process
Monthly expanding retraining with fixed pre-2026 isotonic calibration. All features are available before first pitch.
H3 weights
Nonnegative count-model weights sum to one and are shrunk toward equal weighting. Regularization was chosen by expanding-date 2025 log loss; monthly 2026 weights see settled prior games only.
Uncertainty edge
At T−12h, conservative edge equals signed raw edge minus one ensemble probability standard deviation. A wager must retain at least 8pp after this penalty.
Data split
2022–2024 model development; 2025 strategy validation; 2026 chronological walk-forward through July 10.
Odds
Real OddsAPI sportsbook snapshots. Consensus no-vig probability defines market disagreement; best available quoted price determines P&L; close is used only for CLV.
Execution
T−12h morning board, with T−6h fallback only when the earlier market is absent. One bet per pitcher-game.
Selection warning
The band, H3 router, and uncertainty rule were reviewed after completed 2026 results. They are paper challengers, not untouched evidence.
Deployment
Real-money stakes remain forcibly disabled. A new frozen post–July 10 window must confirm positive ROI and CLV before promotion.
